KEY TAKEAWAY
================================================================================
Exp 1 (LAQA + MRL both enabled) achieves the best scores across every RAG
quality metric (retrieval, faithfulness, answer quality, hallucination safety),
but has the highest latency across all three latency categories. This is a
clear quality-vs-speed tradeoff as components (LAQA, MRL) are removed across
Exp1 -> Exp4.

Exp 5 (Direct LLM, no retrieval/agent pipeline) is fastest by far (~14.6ms total
vs 40-49ms for the RAG variants) and shows zero hallucination rate, but scores
much lower on lexical overlap (BLEU/ROUGE/F1), Grounded Answers (0.0000), and
Answer Quality (0.80 vs 6.70-8.70), and was evaluated on a much smaller sample
(20 questions vs 200). It also introduces a new S.C.O.P.E judge score
(Weighted Total 1.68/5.00) not computed for the other experiments, so it is
not directly comparable on that dimension. Note Grounded Answers = 0.0000
alongside Hallucination Safe = 1.0000 is an apparent inconsistency in the
source report worth double-checking before drawing conclusions.
